How it works

  1. Scan the barcode

    Point the camera at a packaged food. It reads on its own, with no button to press, or you can type the numbers under the bars.

  2. Read it per 100 kcal

    Protein, carbohydrate and fat in grams per 100 kcal, each one's share of the energy, and the label figures to check against the pack.

  3. Keep and compare

    Everything you look up is saved in History. Star the products you buy again, and put two or more side by side.

FAQ

Why per 100 kcal?

Labels give nutrition per 100 g or per serving, which mixes two questions: how much energy a food carries, and what that energy is made of. Per 100 kcal answers the second one on its own and puts every product on the same scale.

100 kcal holds at most about 25 g of protein, 25 g of carbohydrate or 11 g of fat, so every reading falls in a small range you quickly learn to read. 18 / 7 / 0 is almost all protein; 7 / 10 / 4 is a mix of all three.

Where do the figures come from?

From Open Food Facts, a public food database built by volunteers. Kcal Lens reads from it and has no catalogue of its own. Entries can be wrong, out of date or based on a different regional recipe, so the label figures are always shown next to the result for you to check against the pack.

It says there is no entry for this barcode

Open Food Facts has gaps, most often supermarket own-brands and products from smaller markets. Choose Enter manually and copy the energy, protein, carbohydrate and fat from the label. The product is saved on your phone and opens straight away the next time you scan it.

If the product is added to Open Food Facts later, its figures replace the ones you typed. Your name for it and your star are kept.

Why don’t the numbers add up to exactly 100 kcal?

Fibre, alcohol and sugar alcohols carry energy that the three macros don’t account for, and label values are rounded. Small differences are expected.

Can I use it without the camera?

Yes. You can type the numbers printed under any barcode, or enter a product from its label. If you turn camera access off, the app stays fully usable.

Does it work offline?

Looking up a new product needs a connection. History, Favourites and every product you have saved work offline, from the copy stored on your phone.

Is anything I type sent anywhere?

No. There is no account, your lists live only on your phone, and your own product names and label figures are never sent anywhere. The only network traffic is the barcode lookup and product photos from Open Food Facts. The full detail is in the privacy policy.

Kcal Lens reports label data and rearranges it. It does not rate products or give nutritional or medical advice.
