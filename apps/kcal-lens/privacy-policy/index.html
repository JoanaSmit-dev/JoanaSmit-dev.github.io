Introduction

Kcal Lens ("we", "our", or "us") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how our iPhone application ("App") handles your information.

In short: the App has no account, no analytics and no advertising. The products you look up and everything you type stay on your iPhone. The only thing the App sends over the internet is the barcode of a product you look up, to the public food database Open Food Facts.

Camera

The App asks for access to your camera to read barcodes. The camera view is analysed on your device only to find a barcode. No photos or video are taken, saved or sent anywhere.

The camera is optional. If you don’t allow it, you can type the numbers under a barcode or enter a product from its label instead. You can change camera access at any time in Settings → Kcal Lens.

Product Lookups

When you scan or type a barcode, the App sends that barcode to Open Food Facts, a public database of food products built by volunteers, and receives the product’s name, nutrition figures and photo. This needs an internet connection.

As with any request over the internet, Open Food Facts receives your device’s IP address along with the barcode. The App doesn’t send any account, name, device identifier or other personal information, and we don’t receive or store any part of these lookups. Open Food Facts handles this data under its own privacy policy.

Your own product names and any figures you type in from a label are never sent to Open Food Facts or anywhere else.

Data Stored on Your iPhone

The App keeps the following on your iPhone only, so that your lists work offline:

  • Every product you look up (History), with its name, nutrition figures and photo
  • The products you star (Favourites)
  • Names you give products, and figures you enter from a label
  • Your app settings, such as whether you have seen the welcome screen

This data is not stored in any cloud service we operate, and we can’t see it. If you back up your iPhone to iCloud or a computer, it may be included in that backup, as for any app.

You can remove a product with Remove from history, which deletes it everywhere in the App, including Favourites and any figures you entered. Deleting the App removes all of its data from your iPhone.

Third-Party Services

Apart from Open Food Facts, described above, the App uses no third-party services. It contains no advertising, analytics or tracking, and it does not share data with data brokers or advertisers.

If you have chosen in your iPhone’s settings to share analytics with app developers, Apple may send us anonymised crash reports and usage statistics. These are controlled by you in Settings → Privacy & Security → Analytics & Improvements, and are handled under Apple’s privacy policy.

Disclaimer

The App reports nutrition data from Open Food Facts, or figures you enter, and rearranges it per 100 kcal. Database entries are contributed by volunteers and can be wrong, out of date or based on a different recipe. The App does not rate products or give nutritional, dietary or medical advice. Consult a doctor or registered dietitian for personal guidance.

Children's Privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

Your Rights

What we hold. We operate no servers, accounts or analytics of our own. Your lists stay on your iPhone and we have no means of identifying you. There is therefore no personal data held by us to access, correct, export or delete.

What Open Food Facts receives. The barcodes you look up and your IP address, as described under Product Lookups. Requests concerning that data should be directed to Open Food Facts.

Controls available to you now:

  • Turn camera access off in Settings → Kcal Lens.
  • Remove individual products with Remove from history.
  • Delete the App to remove all of its data from your iPhone.

Statutory rights. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, the GDPR gives you the right to access your personal data, to have it corrected or erased, to restrict or object to its processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent where processing relies on it. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your national data protection authority.

To exercise any of these rights in respect of data we hold, contact us at o.j.smitaite@gmail.com. As we hold no personal data, we will normally only be able to confirm that none exists.
